# New Earth – Fun Review v0.6

## Design focus
v0.6 deliberately does not import subsystems from other games wholesale. It strengthens New Earth's own core loop: invest on a dying Earth, choose what survives the transition, then enjoy a rewarding second-act landing and spatial colony game.

## Changes
- Earth catastrophes are telegraphed by threatened regions before the next event.
- Launch is a milestone: landing rover, immediate survey, new research, and (with 4+ settlers) two starting settlements.
- Landing turn gets a small action/science burst so the second act starts with choices rather than waiting.
- New Earth basic buildings are cheaper to reduce dead turns.
- Terrain position matters more: canyon mines, ice farms, and labs near analyzed ruins gain network bonuses.
- Three missions are selected per game from five possible colony goals. They provide alternate routes to 10 SP.
- Technologies, experts and projects have readable synergy tags; tags are descriptive connectors, not a new currency.
- Alien research stays small (four technologies) and is only unlocked after landing.

## Current design judgement
The strongest identity is the transition itself. Earth should feel like optimization under a visible deadline; New Earth should feel like reward, discovery and improvisation. Future additions should be rejected unless they reinforce that contrast.
